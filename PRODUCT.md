# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Analytics engineers, data operators, and business users who need guarded answers from warehouse data without hand-writing every SQL query. They use the app in a work context, often validating the generated SQL, result evidence, and metadata before trusting an answer.

## Product Purpose

NL2SQL Pro turns natural-language business questions into scoped, guarded SQL, executes against DuckDB or ClickHouse, explains the retrieval and guard path, and lets operators maintain the semantic metadata that keeps answers reliable. Success means users can ask, inspect, correct, and govern analytics workflows with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Careful, capable, and ledger-like. The product should feel precise and work-focused, with enough craft to earn trust but no decorative flourish that competes with data inspection.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards with blue-purple gradients, oversized marketing heroes, ornamental glass panels, playful data gimmicks, and UI that hides SQL/evidence behind vague assistant magic. Avoid localizing or stylizing domain metadata as if it were chrome.

## Design Principles

- Evidence before flourish: SQL, guard state, retrieval sources, and result shape must remain easy to scan.
- Preserve operator control: destructive, uncertain, or expensive actions need clear states and recovery paths.
- Localize presentation only: UI language can change, but input understanding and business metadata stay stable.
- Dense but calm: support repeated analytical work with restrained hierarchy, predictable controls, and compact details.
- Trust through consistency: the same visual vocabulary should apply across chat, results, metadata admin, and validation surfaces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast and keyboard operation. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Keep controls legible in both Chinese and English, and avoid relying on color alone for guard, status, and retrieval states.
